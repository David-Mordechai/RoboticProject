# Session summary: 2026-10-27 (project kickoff)

## Goal
Get hands-on, real-world robotics experience on 3 small ground robots, and use it to build the framework and know-how for autonomous AI agents that will later run a **UAV fleet**. Missions should run autonomously, fast, accurately and intelligently, with self-learning.

The original Gemini plan (`autonomous_swarm_robotics_project_plan.md`) is a starting idea only, not a requirement.

## Hardware (ordered, in shipping)
- 3x **Adeept 4WD Mecanum Smart Car kit, ADR036** (Robot HAT V3.2, camera, ultrasonic, line + light tracking, pan/tilt servos, 4x TT motors, OLED, 8x16 LED matrix, 2x18650 holder)
- 3x **Raspberry Pi 5 8GB**, 3x SanDisk Extreme 128GB A2 microSD
- No LiDAR yet

## What we learned

### The kit ([research/01](research/01-adeept-mecanum-kit.md))
- Code: <https://github.com/adeept/Adeept_4WD_Smart_Car_for_RPi>. **Clone from GitHub, not the zip**: GitHub has a Pi 5 fix from Sept 2026. **There's no license**, so we write our own drivers.
- Robot HAT V3.2:
  - PCA9685 PWM @ I2C 0x5F (servos ch0–7, motors ch8–15, 50 Hz)
  - DRV8833 motor drivers
  - ADS7830 ADC @ 0x48 (battery, light sensor)
  - OLED @ 0x3C, LED matrix @ 0x70
  - **An empty MPU6050 (IMU) socket**
- **No wheel encoders and no IMU.** The robot can't measure where it is: this is the biggest gap for autonomy.
- **Pi 5 power is the #1 risk.** The HAT powers the Pi through the GPIO 5V pins from an unrated regulator (the V3.3 board has 2x XL4015). On GPIO power the Pi 5 also limits USB to 600 mA. Test with `vcgencmd get_throttled` while the servos and motors move.
- Fleet defaults are identical and insecure: hotspot SSID/password, `admin:123456`, everything runs as root. Change them per robot.
- **The 2 main plates:**
  - The lower plate A02 carries the motors.
  - The upper plate A01 sits 30 mm higher on 8 copper standoffs.
  - The battery holder hangs between the two plates.
  - The Pi stack sits on A01, and top plate A07 sits on 50 mm nylon standoffs.

### Raspberry Pi 5 ([research/02](research/02-raspberry-pi5-robotics.md))
- Needs an Active Cooler. Uses a 22-pin camera cable. RPi.GPIO doesn't work, so use gpiozero/lgpio. Use rmw_zenoh for the Wi-Fi fleet.
- OS decision: **Raspberry Pi OS + ROS 2 Jazzy in Docker**.
- The Pi 5 CPU alone runs YOLO26n at about 15 FPS, and 1.5B LLMs at about 10 tok/s. If we add an AI HAT, the Hailo-8 is better than the AI HAT+ 2 for vision.

### Architecture and roadmap ([research/03](research/03-autonomy-architecture-and-roadmap.md), [research/00](research/00-overview.md))
- **Skill API** (NavigateTo, Explore, Search, ReturnHome…) as the boundary between vehicle-specific code and everything else. Everything above it carries over to UAVs.
- **The LLM proposes, a deterministic layer verifies and executes.** The LLM never drives motors.
- **Centralized planning, decentralized execution.** Contract Net first, then CBBA.
- **Self-improvement** = a fixed benchmark + automated tuning + LLM proposals that must pass the benchmark first.
- .NET for the ground station; Python/C++ on the robots. PX4 for UAVs later.
- 10-phase roadmap with KPIs for "fast, accurate, intelligent".

### Fit checks in CAD (`hardware/cad/`, `docs/images/cad/`)
- **The Active Cooler fits under the Robot HAT.**
  - The HAT sits on 21 mm standoffs. The cooler is 10.5 mm tall above the Pi; its 13.7 mm "overall" height includes 3.2 mm of clip below the board.
  - Clearance: **8.5 mm over the fan**, **~3.3 mm at the tightest spot** (the motor connectors under the HAT above a cooler clip).
  - Every underside part is now modelled: the GPIO socket, 4 motor connectors, DC-DC regulators and coils, and the chips.
- **LiDAR (RPLIDAR C1):**
  - The laser plane is 29.8 mm above its base.
  - The camera head's top is ~83 mm above A01 (~85 mm when tilted), about 30 mm above the top plate.
  - Directly on the plate, the beam is blocked. **Use 15 mm M2.5 standoffs** (beam ~13 mm above the head). The screws into the LiDAR must be ≤ 4 mm.
- The chassis sizes are estimated from Adeept's CAD views (±5 mm). **Measure the real kit** and update `D` in `hardware/cad/robot_model.py`.

### Tools set up
- **FreeCAD 1.1 + freecad-mcp**: Claude drives FreeCAD. Setup: `scripts/setup-dev-windows.ps1`, guide: [setup/cad-freecad-mcp.md](setup/cad-freecad-mcp.md). Auto-start RPC server is on.
- `hardware/cad/`:
  - `robot_model.py`: parametric model of the robot and board stack.
  - `drawings.py`: dimensioned 2D drawings.
  - `render_views.py`: product-style stills.
  - `stack_animation.py`: the assembly GIF.
  - `save_models.py`: saves `.FCStd`/`.step` to `out/`.
  - `fc.py`: the RPC client.
- FreeCAD navigation is set to Gesture: left-drag rotates, right-drag pans, the wheel zooms.
