## 3. The fixes (in the repo, not yet on the car)
1. **Depth floor fix** (`car/perception/depth_scan.py`): fit the floor plane in every frame and keep only points
   ≥ 3 cm above it; a direction counts only if ≥ 2 points agree. Synthetic test with the measured 1.2° tilt
   (`car/perception/test/test_floor_fit.py`): false floor directions 141/145 → **0**, chair base seen 0% → **97%**.
   Also logs its own CPU time per frame, to find why it used a whole core.
2. **Behaviour tree B** (`car/nav/navigate_ackermann_b.xml`): replan only when the route becomes blocked or the goal
   changes, not every second. Recoveries: clear the maps, then wait 2 s, and only third a short 0.15 m back-up.
3. **Trip script** (`car/nav/nav_trip.py`): no extra back-up of its own; 150 s per goal.
4. **Parking** (`car/nav/nav_a_home.sh`): plan the return straight to the spot in front of the charger, facing out,
   with the heading planned from the hall; then reverse in on the rear LiDAR (`car/nav/dock.py`).
5. Bring-up fixes from today, already used on the car: LiDAR topic, footprint from the URDF, YAML aliases, Humble
   polygon format, through-poses tree, "approach" safety zone (the "stop" box held the car in the charger),
   verified start pose, minimum speed above the motor dead band.
